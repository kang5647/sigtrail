Counting rules and coverage

One request = one distinct retained receipt containing an exact sign FunctionCall, filtered to v1.signer during collection. Responses, internal completion and other methods are excluded.

Parent inclusion defines the cohort. Descendant receipts may fall outside the day. Older normalized records retain creation timestamps, not execution timestamps, and omit raw receivers and outcomes. Provider completeness has not been independently established against a full node.

Direct-stage fees and sensitivity assumptions
All scenario requests count; operator independence is unknown. Assumes constant sampled fees and protocol rules. Excludes parent transactions, responses, cleanup, other contracts and destination fees. This is not a forecast, service revenue or token price model. Intents fees and buybacks are separate.

Further thesis boundaries
More persuasive adoption evidence would include repeated complete-day cohorts over several weeks, independently identified unrelated app operators, linked destination outcomes and complete NEAR workflow fees. Declining account concentration alone would not prove broader adoption.
Utility depends on signing-service security and availability. Outbound signing does not independently verify inbound chain state. This research does not establish superiority over other systems or a token price implication.
