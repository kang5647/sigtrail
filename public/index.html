03 / The investment thesis

Our answer is conditional.

003
THE CLAIM

Chain Signatures make a NEAR account useful on other chains. A durable platform thesis requires independent applications to use that primitive repeatedly—and a credible path from that activity to NEAR-token value.

Why this primitive is NEAR-specific ↗
A

What would support it

More direct sign requests across comparable windows, with a falling share from the largest caller and more independent initiating contracts.

B

What would weaken it

Headline activity rising only because one bridge grows, callbacks multiply, or requests never connect to durable fee capture.

C

What we cannot claim

A sign receipt is not proof that a transaction settled on Bitcoin, Ethereum, or another chain. Caller accounts are not users. Three samples cannot establish a long-term trend.

04 / Method

Re-run the question.

We fixed block heights 200,000,000, 212,000,000, and 224,000,000 before classifying methods. Each window spans 1,000 blocks; these are not random samples. We fetch matching transactions involving v1.signer, hydrate their receipts, deduplicate receipt IDs, and count only sign calls addressed to that contract.

Economic context aligns complete UTC days across DeFiLlama fee, revenue, holder revenue, and NEAR DeFi TVL feeds. Current partial day is excluded. Method and limitations are in the repository.
