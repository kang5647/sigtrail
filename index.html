03 / The investment thesis

Two tests, one conditional thesis.

003
ADOPTION TEST

Chain Signatures are used. In the latest window, three initiating accounts made 90% of direct requests. Across the sampled day, the pooled top three made 82.3%. Broad independent application demand remains unproven.

Why this primitive is NEAR-specific ↗
A

What would support adoption

More direct sign requests across comparable windows, with a falling top-three share and more independently identified applications.

B

Burn is measurable; material impact is unproven

A direct sign receipt averaged 0.000419 NEAR in supply burn at protocol v88. The editable panel compares this sign-stage amount with observed gross issuance; the complete workflow remains unmeasured. Intents buybacks are unrelated to this receipt sample.

C

What we cannot claim

A sign receipt does not prove outbound settlement. Caller accounts are not users. Spaced sampling across one day and seven historical windows cannot establish a long-term trend.

04 / Method

Re-run the question.

We audit seven fixed 1,000-block historical ranges from block 200,000,000 to 224,000,999, four million block heights apart. A second audit samples twelve 1,000-block ranges across 11 November UTC at a fixed 12,000-block cadence. These are not random or full-day censuses. We hydrate every matching transaction's receipts, deduplicate receipt IDs, and count only sign calls addressed to v1.signer.

Economic context aligns complete UTC days across DeFiLlama fee, revenue, holder revenue, and NEAR DeFi TVL feeds. Current partial day is excluded. The protocol economics and committed snapshot anchor the burn-versus-issuance comparison. Detailed sampling and ownership limits are in METHODOLOGY.md in the repository.
