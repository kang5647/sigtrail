# Live demo, 60 seconds

Record the **actual dashboard**, at `http://localhost:4177`, without slides or continuous page motion. Keep the cursor still between clicks. The voiceover should distinguish evidence from inference.

1. **0–8 s — Question.** Show the hero. “NEAR can sign for other chains. But are many apps using that primitive, or are explorers counting contract plumbing?”
2. **8–25 s — The reveal.** Click **Explore the evidence**. In the November window, show **170 transactions touching signer** versus **80 direct sign requests**. Point briefly to the receipt breakdown. “We classified actual function-call receipts. MPC responses and internal completions are separate work.”
3. **25–39 s — Who uses it?** Scroll to **Application mix**. Show `omni.bridge.near` at **43 of 80 requests**, or **53.8%**. “One bridge contract accounts for more than half this sampled window.”
4. **39–48 s — Verify.** Click a row under **Open the proof** and show the public transaction trace in NearBlocks. Return to the app.
5. **48–60 s — Judgment.** Show **Our answer is conditional**. “The primitive is real. Broad, durable application demand is still unproven by these samples. Here is the data and one command to rerun it.”

The figures are from the committed 12 November 2026 UTC snapshot. If refreshing the data changes them, update the narration. Do not describe these windows as a growth trend or a random sample.
